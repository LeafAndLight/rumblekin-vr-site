Hands-on chaos

Your hands
set it off.

Choose an ability from the wrist interface. Aim either tracked hand and use its assigned trigger; movement stays arm-driven, with Grab & Hook for latching and pulling.

01

Super Punch

Release a short-range super punch around either hand. Regular punches remain available during cooldown.

02

Super Jump

Launch from nearby ground or a firm handhold. Find fresh support before jumping again.

03

Quick Roll

Trigger a quick automatic burst of movement. Hand attacks stay available.

04

Fireball

At Form 3, aim a tracked hand and launch an explosive fireball.

05

Shockwave

Point either hand and release a full-power shockwave.

Movement tool

Grab & Hook

A tendril launches from the palm and latches onto a surface. Hold to pull; release to let go.
